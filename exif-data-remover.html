EXIF Metadata & GPS Privacy Leak: Deep Technical Guide

Jab bhi aap kisi iPhone, Android smartphone ya DSLR camera se photo click karte hain, camera ka internal sensor keval visual pixels record nahi karta. Us photo ke header me ek confidential binary structure jod diya jata hai jise EXIF (Exchangeable Image File Format) kaha jata hai. Iske standard guidelines Camera & Imaging Products Association (CIPA) aur ISO Specifications dwara banaye gaye hain.

Is metadata me aisi jankari shamil hoti hai jo anjane me aapki personal security aur privacy ko khatre me daal sakti hai:

  • Exact GPS Coordinates (अक्षांश और देशांतर): Photo me aapke ghar, school, hotel ya office ka exact latitude aur longitude darj hota hai jise Google Maps par daal kar koi bhi aapka pata nikal sakta hai.
  • Camera & Lens Fingerprint: Device ka model (jaise iPhone 15 Pro, Samsung Galaxy S24 Ultra), shutter count aur lens serial number jo cyber profiling me kaam aate hain.
  • High-Resolution Timestamps: Kis tareekh aur kis second par photo li gayi thi.
  • Owner & Copyright Tags: Adobe Photoshop ya software editing history ke traces.
