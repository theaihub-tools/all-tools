What is the Online Days Calculator?

The Days Calculator by The AI Hub Tools is an all-in-one date calculation platform designed for project managers, event planners, legal researchers, students, and freelancers. It lets you measure the exact span between dates or project future and past deadlines by adding or subtracting calendar days, working days, weeks, and months.

How to Use This Tool

  1. Select Your Mode: Choose Date Difference to find the gap between two dates, or Add / Subtract Days to project deadlines.
  2. Choose Dates: Select start and end points. The tool automatically displays the matching Day of the Week (e.g., Monday, Friday).
  3. Business Days Mode: When projecting deadlines, check Business days only to skip Saturdays and Sundays automatically.
  4. Calendar Export: Click Add to Calendar (.ics) to export target dates straight into Google Calendar or Outlook.

Key Features

  • Multi-Unit Projection: Add or subtract combinations of Years, Months, Weeks, and Days.
  • Safe Month Calculation: Zero month-overflow errors on 28th/31st day transitions.
  • Business Days Isolation: Accurately calculate workdays excluding weekends for sprint planning.
  • Day of the Week Detection: Real-time display of weekday names for every selected date.
  • Calendar (.ics) Download: One-click event creation for Google, Apple, and Outlook calendars.
  • Timezone-Safe Engine: Native local date parsing eliminates 1-day shifting errors across global timezones.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

1. Is this Days Calculator completely free?

Yes, this tool is 100% free with unlimited calculations, no subscription, and no sign-up required.

2. How does the "Business Days Only" mode work?

When enabled in Add/Subtract mode, the engine steps forward or backward one day at a time, entirely skipping Saturdays and Sundays until the specified business day count is satisfied.

3. Does this calculator consider leap years?

Yes, all leap year calculations (including February 29th) are handled accurately by browser-native Gregorian calendar parsing.

4. What does the (.ics) download button do?

It generates an industry-standard iCalendar file that automatically opens in Google Calendar, Apple Calendar, or Outlook to set a reminder for your target deadline.
