What is the Case Converter & Word Counter?

This is a fast, lightweight, and completely private online text utility designed for content writers, bloggers, students, and software developers. Effortlessly switch between uppercase, lowercase, title case, sentence case, alternate case, and URL-friendly slug format while tracking words, characters, paragraphs, and reading time in real time.

How to Use This Tool

  1. Input Content: Type, paste, or click the Paste button to populate your text in the workspace.
  2. Choose Formatting: Select any case option such as UPPERCASE, lowercase, Title Case, Sentence case, or slug-case.
  3. Clean Formatting: Click Clean Spaces to instantly eliminate unnecessary double spaces and tabs.
  4. Revert Anytime: Accidentally made an unwanted change? Use Undo to revert back to your previous version.
  5. Copy or Export: Click Copy Text to copy it directly to your clipboard or Download (.txt) to save it locally.

Key Features

  • Live Real-time Counter: Instant calculations for words, characters without spaces, lines, and reading duration.
  • 100% Client-Side Privacy: No data is ever transmitted or stored on remote servers; all processing is local.
  • SEO Friendly Slugs: Automatically sanitize article headers and titles into search-optimized hyphenated slugs.
  • Undo & Redo Protection: Never lose your original draft with built-in state memory.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

1. Is this Case Converter & Word Counter completely free?

Yes, this tool is 100% free with unlimited usage and requires no registration, installation, or subscription.

2. What is the difference between Title Case and Sentence case?

Title Case capitalizes the initial letter of every major word, whereas Sentence case only capitalizes the first letter of each complete sentence.

3. Can I recover my draft if I make a mistake?

Yes, simply press the Undo button to instantly restore your previous version step-by-step.

4. Is my private text secure?

Absolutely. The entire application runs inside your browser using JavaScript. Your text is never uploaded to any cloud server or database.

5. Is there an input word limit?

No, there are no character or word limitations. You can format short code snippets or lengthy 50,000-word manuscripts without delay.
