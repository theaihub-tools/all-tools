Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

What makes a prompt photorealistic in AI image generators?

Photorealism comes from specifying physical optics rather than generic buzzwords: sensor noise, shallow depth of field (f/1.4 to f/2.8), authentic lens focal lengths (35mm or 85mm), and physical lighting behaviors such as subsurface scattering on skin and volumetric shadows.

Why do I need a negative prompt for Stable Diffusion and Flux?

Negative prompts tell the neural network which visual patterns to penalize during latent denoising steps, eliminating deformed fingers, duplicate heads, blurred details, and unwanted signatures.

Does this tool work with Hinglish inputs?

Yes. You can describe your scene in simple Hindi or Hinglish, and the enhancer will structure the output with international English terminology that global AI models process accurately.
