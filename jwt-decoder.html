Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

    

      
1. Are my JWT tokens, secrets, or PEM keys uploaded to any server?

      
No. JWT Decoder Pro runs 100% client-side in your browser RAM. Zero tokens or secret keys are ever transmitted over the network.

    

    

      
2. Does it support asymmetric RS256 and ES256 public key verification?

      
Yes! Switch the verifier dropdown to RSA / ECDSA Public Key (PEM) or JWKS JSON / Endpoint URL to verify asymmetric tokens from Auth0, Firebase, Cognito, or custom OAuth servers.

    

    

      
3. How does the Security Lab weak-secret check work?

      
It tests your loaded HMAC token locally against a built-in dictionary of common default secrets (like secret, 123456, changeme) in milliseconds to ensure your signing key isn't easily guessable.

    

    

      
4. Can I compare two JWTs to see what changed after a token refresh?

      
Yes. Open the Token Diff tab and paste both tokens to see a claim-by-claim table highlighting added, removed, or modified fields.

    

    

      
5. Can I decode multiple JWTs from server logs at once?

      
Yes! Switch to the Batch Decoder tab, paste up to 100 tokens (one per line), inspect their expiration statuses, and export the results as a CSV spreadsheet.
