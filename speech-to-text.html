The AI Hub Voice Studio: Next-Generation Speech-to-Text Ecosystem

Traditional voice typing tools automatically stop listening after a few seconds of silence, making it frustrating to dictate long speeches, scripts, or articles. The AI Hub Ultimate Voice Studio runs on a browser-native neural speech recognition engine with an intelligent auto-reconnect architecture. It supports real-time cursor insertion, allowing you to place your cursor anywhere inside the document and dictate new sentences seamlessly.

Key Features & How to Use:

• Multi-Speaker Diarization (Interview Mode): Recording an interview or podcast? Simply say "Speaker 1" or "Speaker 2" (or set custom speaker names in the toolbar). The tool automatically inserts the speaker's name, live timestamp, and a clean paragraph break.
• Live Voice Calculator (Voice Math): Say "five hundred plus three hundred" while dictating, and the studio automatically calculates and types 500 + 300 = 800.
• Live Translation Split View: Dictate in Hindi or regional languages on the left while viewing real-time English translation on the right.
• YouTube Teleprompter (with Mirror Mode): Read your dictated script on a fullscreen auto-scrolling teleprompter with adjustable speed and mirror mode for camera rigs.
• AI Content Transformer & Grammar Fixer: Convert raw voice notes into formal emails, YouTube scripts, or SEO blog posts with a single click, and clean up punctuation and grammar effortlessly.

Supported Voice Commands:

Speak these trigger phrases during dictation to insert punctuation and formatting automatically:
- Full stop or Period ➔ . (or । in Hindi)
- Comma ➔ ,
- Question mark ➔ ?
- New line or Next line ➔ Line break (Enter)
- Bullet point ➔ Inserts a new bullet item (•)
