Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

1. Are my photos uploaded to any external server?
No! 100% private. The AI model runs locally inside your web browser, so your personal or product photos never leave your device.

2. How do I make a YouTube thumbnail cutout with a white border?
After removing the background, open the ✨ Outline tab, pick your outline color (white is selected by default), and drag the Sticker Outline slider to your desired thickness.

3. Can I create 1:1 Amazon/Flipkart product photos or Passport photos?
Yes! Open the 📐 Sizes tab to select 1:1 Product, 16:9 YouTube, 9:16 Reels, or Passport ratio, and select a pure white or blue backdrop in the Backdrop tab.

4. What if I make a mistake while using the Magic Brush?
You can use the ↩️ Undo and ↪️ Redo buttons inside the Brush tab at any time, or click Reset Mask to restore the original AI cutout.
