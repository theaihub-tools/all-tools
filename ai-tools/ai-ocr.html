Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

1. Which international languages are supported?
Our AI OCR supports 35+ global languages including English, Chinese (Simplified & Traditional), Japanese, Korean, Russian, French, German, Spanish, Portuguese, Italian, Arabic, Turkish, Indonesian, Vietnamese, Thai, and 10+ Indian languages like Hindi, Sanskrit, Marathi, Gujarati, Bengali, Tamil, and Telugu.

2. Can I scan multiple book pages or receipts at once?
Yes! Select multiple images while uploading—our Batch OCR engine will scan all pages automatically and combine the extracted text page-by-page.

3. What is Otsu Smart Document mode?
Otsu Binarization automatically calculates the optimal brightness threshold of your photo to turn shadowed or colored paper into crisp black text on a pure white background, dramatically boosting accuracy.

4. Are my documents and screenshots kept private?
100% private. All text recognition runs locally inside your web browser—your images are never uploaded to any external server.
